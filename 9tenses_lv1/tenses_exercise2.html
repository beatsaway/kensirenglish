English Tenses Practice (for Cantonese speakers)

There are 12 tenses in English. (新手先練好9種常用時態) [Simple/Continuous/Perfect] x [Present/Past/Future]
新手溫習時態小貼士：
  • 一定先用present講/寫出來，決定好係simple、continuous定perfect tense
  • 再決定有冇必要轉past或future
  • past就轉verb、future就前面加will
Simple Tense 組
我地尋日無學返 We (1) no school yesterday.
我地今日有學返 We (2) school today.
我地遲啲食lunch We (3) lunch later.
ahad
bhave
cwill have
Perfect Tense 組
我仲未做我既art功課 I (1) my art assignment/homework.
我尋晚忘記咗佢 I (2) it last night.
我會最遲lunch搞掂佢 I (3) it by lunch.
ahaven't done
bhad forgotten
cwill have finished
Continuous Tense 組
我地而家就快食緊飯 We (1) soon!
我地玩緊閃避球lunch前 We (2) dodgeball before lunch.
我會係訓緊覺下午數學堂 I (3) during the afternoon maths lesson.
aare eating
bwere playing
cwill be sleeping
Lie 講大話 - Simple Tense
我成日講大話 I (1).
我之前成日講大話 I (2).
我會成日講大話 I (3).
aalways lie
balways lied
cwill always lie
Lie 講大話 - Perfect Tense
我講咗大話 (剛剛) I (1).
我講過大話 (曾經) I (2) before.
我會係講咗十次大話 當你發現嗰陣 I (3) already when you realise.
ahave lied
bhad lied
cwill have lied
Lie 講大話 - Continuous Tense
我講緊大話 I (1).
我頭先講緊大話 I (2).
我會係講緊大話 I (3).
aam lying
bwas lying
cwill be lying
Bath 沖涼 - Simple Tense
我日日訓覺前沖個涼 (habit) I (1) a bath before sleep/bed every day.
我(頭先)沖個涼訓覺前 I (2) a bath before sleep/bed.
我會訓覺前沖個涼 I (3) a bath before sleep/bed.
atake
btook
cwill take
Bath 沖涼 - Continuous Tense
我沖緊個涼 I (1) a bath.
我(頭先)沖緊個涼 I (2) a bath.
我會係沖緊涼十點嗰陣 I (3) a bath at ten.
aam taking
bwas taking
cwill be taking
Bath 沖涼 - Perfect Tense
我沖咗個涼了 I (1) a bath already.
我沖咗個涼了食飯前 I (2) a bath before dinner.
我會係沖咗個涼了食飯前 I (3) a bath already before dinner.
ahave taken
bhad taken
cwill have taken
