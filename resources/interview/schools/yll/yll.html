Interview Format and Practice Questions
面試形式與練習問題

This page summarizes the interview format and key points based on the news article about the Christian Hong Kong Lutheran Yuen Long Lutheran Secondary School (YLL). Use it to understand the process and practice real interview questions. 本頁根據有關基督教香港信義會元朗信義中學（YLL）的新聞文章，整理面試流程及重點，方便了解流程並練習真實面試問題。

Key idea: The interview begins the moment you enter the campus. Teachers observe your manners, greetings, and behavior throughout the visit. 重點： 面試由你踏入校園一刻開始，老師會留意你的禮貌、問候及整段過程的表現。

Interview Format (Two Rounds)
面試形式（兩輪）

Round 1: Students Only 第一輪：只限學生

  • Format: Small group discussion (about 5 students). 形式：小組討論（約5人一組）。
  • Language: Cantonese for group discussion. 語言：小組討論以廣東話進行。
  • Preparation time: About 10 minutes to read materials. 準備時間：約10分鐘閱讀材料。
  • Purpose: Check logic, communication, and math/science thinking. 目的：考核邏輯、溝通及數理思維。
  • English part: Answer daily-life questions in English, with follow-up questions. 英語部分：以英語回答日常生活問題，並有追問。
  • Math/Science part: Topic sent about 24 hours before the interview. 數理部分：面試前約24小時發放題目。
  • You may use any resources while preparing (books, internet, AI, or asking others). 準備時可使用任何資源（書本、網絡、AI或向他人請教）。
  • You may present in Cantonese, English, or Putonghua, using any format. 可用廣東話、英語或普通話匯報，形式不限。

Round 2: Student + Parent 第二輪：學生＋家長

  • Format: Student and parent interview together. 形式：學生與家長一同面試。
  • Purpose: Understand family education values and support. 目的：了解家庭教育理念及支援。
  • School emphasizes "admitting the whole family," not just the student. 學校強調「取錄整個家庭」，不只錄取學生。
  • Parents should be ready to explain their education philosophy and how they guide the child. 家長需準備好說明教育理念及如何引導子女。

Key Points from the News Article
新聞文章重點

  • Two interview rounds are held; all applicants attend Round 1, and about half move to Round 2. 面試分兩輪，所有申請人先參加第一輪，約一半可晉級第二輪。
  • Round 1 checks English ability and math/science thinking in separate parts. 第一輪分別評估英語能力及數理思維。
  • The math/science task has no fixed answer; the process and explanation matter most. 數理題目沒有標準答案，重視思考過程及解釋。
  • The school observes students from the moment they enter campus, including manners and behavior. 學校由學生踏入校園起已開始觀察禮貌與行為表現。
  • The school receives hundreds of applications and offers about 50 places. 每年收到數百份申請，名額約50個。

Application Notes (from school notice)
申請重點（學校通告）

Dates and places are estimates based on the news year noted in the article/school notice. 日期與名額為按新聞／通告年份推算的估計資料。

  • Application period: Jan 2 to Jan 16 (office hours). 申請期：1月2日至1月16日（辦公時間）。
  • Self-allocation places: 50. 自行分配學位名額：50。
  • Required documents: Education Bureau form, school application form with photo, Primary 5 and 6 report cards, activities certificates, stamped reply envelope. 所需文件：教育局申請表、本校入學申請表（附近照）、小五及小六成績表、課外活動獎狀、已貼郵票回郵信封。
  • Applicants may apply to two schools only. 家長只可向兩間中學申請自行分配學位。
  • Selection criteria: Academic 40%, Interview 40%, Activities/Service 10%, Awards 10%. 收生準則：學業40%、面試40%、課外活動/服務10%、獎項10%。

Practice Questions for Students
學生練習問題

English Speaking (daily life topics) 英語口語（生活題）

  • Describe a learning activity you enjoy and why. 描述一個你最喜歡的學習活動並說明原因。
  • Tell us about a book you read recently. What did you learn? 分享你最近讀的一本書，你學到了什麼？
  • What do you do when you face a difficult task? 面對困難任務時，你會怎樣做？
  • Describe a time you helped a classmate. 描述一次你幫助同學的經驗。
  • What is your favorite school subject? Explain with examples. 你最喜歡哪一科？請用例子說明。
  • How do you manage your time after school? 放學後你如何管理時間？
  • What kind of student do you want to be in secondary school? 你希望在中學成為怎樣的學生？
  • What makes a good team member? 優秀的團隊成員應具備什麼特質？
  • Share a hobby and how you improved at it. 分享一個興趣，以及你如何進步。
  • If you could improve your school, what would you change? 如果可以改善學校，你會改變什麼？

Group Discussion (Cantonese) 小組討論（廣東話）

  • Summary Given a short article, summarize the main point in one minute. 給你一篇短文，1分鐘內總結重點。
  • Decision Your group must decide the best solution to a simple problem. How will you divide tasks and reach agreement? 小組要為一個簡單問題作出最佳方案，你們會如何分工並達成共識？
  • Debate Discuss: "Should students have more group projects than homework?" 討論：「學生應否有更多小組專題少些功課？」
  • Debate Discuss: "What is more important in school: grades or character?" 討論：「成績與品格，哪個更重要？」
  • Improvement Each member presents one idea to improve the school environment. 每位成員提出一個改善校園環境的建議。
  • Q&A You have 10 minutes to read a passage. Share one question and one answer to the group. 有10分鐘閱讀一段文章，向小組分享一條問題與一個答案。
  • Planning Your group must plan a class activity with a small budget. Decide the activity and explain why. 小組用有限預算策劃班級活動，決定活動並解釋原因。
  • Debate Discuss: "Should students use AI tools for homework?" 討論：「學生做功課應否使用AI工具？」
  • Decision Your group must choose one charity to support this month. How will you decide? 小組要選一個慈善機構作本月支援，你們會如何決定？
  • Debate Discuss: "Is daily exercise more important than extra study time?" 討論：「每日運動是否比加時溫書更重要？」
  • Policy Plan a simple class rule to improve punctuality. Explain how it works. 設計一條簡單班規改善準時，並解釋如何運作。
  • Debate Discuss: "Should schools ban smartphones during class?" 討論：「學校上課期間應否禁止使用手機？」
  • Planning Plan a one-week study schedule for exam week. Agree on the top three priorities. 為考試週設計一個一週溫習時間表，商量三大優先事項。
  • Collaboration Assign roles for a group presentation and set a timeline. 分配小組報告角色，並訂立時間表。
  • Problem-Solving The school library is too noisy. Propose solutions and choose the best one. 圖書館太嘈吵，提出解決方案並選出最佳方案。
  • Evaluation Compare three after-school activities and choose the best for student well-being. 比較三項課後活動，選出對學生身心最好的方案。
  • Ethics You find a lost wallet on campus. Decide what to do and explain why. 在校內拾到錢包，你會怎樣做？說明原因。
  • Policy Discuss: "Should students wear school uniforms every day?" 討論：「學生是否每天都要穿校服？」
  • Planning Plan a class service project with a $100 budget. Explain the impact. 用100元預算策劃班級服務活動，說明成效。
  • Opinion What makes a good class leader? Agree on the top three qualities. 好班長應具備甚麼條件？商量三個最重要特質。
  • Decision Choose between a museum trip and a science park trip. Explain your reasons. 在博物館與科學館郊遊之間作選擇，並解釋原因。
  • Debate Discuss: "Should students start school later in the morning?" 討論：「學生早上應否遲啲返學？」

Math and Science Task (prepared 24 hours before) 數理任務（面試前24小時準備）

  • Design Design a safe way to catch a wild boar. Explain the science and safety concerns. 設計一個安全捕捉野豬的方法，說明原理及安全考慮。
  • Optimization How can you cook 11 sausages as fast as possible on a barbecue? Explain your strategy. 如何以最快方法在燒烤爐上烤11條香腸？請解釋策略。
  • Design Design a simple water filter using common materials. Explain how it works. 用常見材料設計簡易濾水器，說明運作原理。
  • Structure Build a paper bridge that holds the most weight. What shapes help? 建造一條可承受最大重量的紙橋，哪些形狀有幫助？
  • Experiment Plan a small experiment to test which surface makes a toy car roll fastest. 設計一個小實驗，測試哪種表面令玩具車滾動最快。
  • Estimation Estimate how many cups of water a classroom sink can hold. Explain your method. 估算課室水盆能盛多少杯水，並解釋方法。
  • Comparison Use math to compare two plans for saving electricity at home. 用數學比較兩個家居節能方案。
  • Measurement Explain how you would measure the height of a tree without climbing it. 不爬樹的情況下，如何量度樹高？
  • Design Design a simple alarm system for a door using basic materials. 用基本材料設計一個簡易門警報系統。
  • Sustainability Create a plan to reduce food waste in the school canteen. Include numbers. 制定校園餐廳減少食物浪費的計劃，包含數據。
  • Design Design a simple system to keep drinks cold without electricity. Explain the science. 設計一個不用電力也能保冷飲品的簡易方法，解釋原理。
  • Estimation Estimate how many books can fit on a classroom shelf. Explain your method. 估算課室書架能放多少本書，並解釋方法。
  • Comparison Compare two routes to school using time and distance. Which is better? 以時間和距離比較兩條返學路線，哪條更好？
  • Design Design a simple wind-powered device using household materials. Explain how it works. 用家居材料設計簡易風力裝置，並解釋運作原理。
  • Experiment Plan a fair test to compare the strength of two paper types. 設計公平測試，比較兩種紙張的強度。
  • Measurement Estimate the area of your classroom using steps and a simple scale. 用步數和比例估算課室面積。
  • Estimation Estimate how many floor tiles are on the school corridor. Explain your method. 估算走廊共有多少塊地磚，並解釋方法。
  • Experiment Plan a test to find which cup keeps a drink warm the longest. 設計測試找出哪種杯子保溫最久。
  • Structure Design a simple earthquake-proof tower using straws and tape. 用飲管和膠紙設計抗震小塔。
  • Optimization Plan the fastest way to distribute 30 lunch boxes to a class. 設計最快方法向全班派發30份飯盒。
  • Calculation Calculate how much paint is needed to paint the classroom walls. 計算粉刷課室牆壁所需油漆量。
  • Data Make a one-week plan to measure class trash weight and reduce it. 制定一週量度班級垃圾重量並減量的計劃。
  • Physics Design a balloon-powered car and explain the forces involved. 設計氣球車並解釋相關力量。
  • Comparison Compare two box shapes to see which holds more with the same material. 比較兩種盒子形狀，用相同材料哪個容量較大。
  • Survey Plan a survey on how students travel to school and present the results. 設計調查了解同學返學方式，並展示結果。

Follow-up Questions (to test thinking) 追問問題（考察思考）

  • Why did you choose this method? 為何選擇這個方法？
  • What would you change if you had more time? 如果有更多時間，你會改變什麼？
  • What if your first plan fails? 如果第一個方案失敗，你會怎樣做？
  • How did you check your answer? 你如何檢查你的答案？
  • Which part was the most difficult, and why? 哪一部分最困難？為什麼？

Parent Interview Practice Questions
家長面試練習問題

  • What is your education philosophy for your child? 你對子女的教育理念是什麼？
  • How do you support your child when they struggle? 子女遇到困難時，你會如何支援？
  • How do you guide your child to be polite and respectful? 你如何培養子女有禮貌及尊重他人？
  • Do you have regular family time (We Time)? What do you do? 你們有固定家庭時間（We Time）嗎？會做什麼？
  • How do you handle mistakes or discipline at home? 在家中你如何處理犯錯或管教？
  • What kind of secondary school environment do you want for your child? 你期望子女在怎樣的中學環境學習？
  • How do you balance academics and character development? 你如何平衡學業與品格培養？
  • How do you encourage reading or independent learning at home? 你如何在家中鼓勵閱讀或自主學習？
Practice tip: Focus on clear thinking and explaining your process. The school values how students reach answers, not just the final answer. 練習貼士：著重清晰思考與解釋過程，學校更看重學生如何得出答案，而不只是答案本身。
