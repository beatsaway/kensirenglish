The 12 Tenses 十二時式

You've learnt the 9 tenses. Perfect Continuous Tenses are the last 3. 你已學過9隻時式，英文12隻最後嘅3隻係Perfect Continuous。

Simple
簡單式		 Continuous
進行式		 Perfect
完成式		 Perfect Continuous
完成進行式
Present
現在		 I eat
我食		 I am eating
我食緊		 I have eaten
我食咗		 I have been eating
我一直食緊
Past
過去		 I ate
(當時)我食		 I was eating
(當時)我食緊		 I had eaten
(當時)我食咗		 I had been eating
(當時)我一直食緊
Future
將來		 I will eat
(到時)我會食		 I will be eating
(到時)我會係食緊		 I will have eaten
(到時)我會食咗		 I will have been eating
(到時)我會一直食緊

Perfect Continuous Tenses describe actions that last for a period of time into the past/present/future. 完成進行式:強調一直做緊嘅嘢維持到 過去/現在/未來 某個時間點。
