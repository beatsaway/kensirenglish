英文時式共12種

Simple Continuous Perfect Perfect Continuous
Present I eat
我食		 I am eating
我食緊		 I have eaten
我食咗		 I have been eating
我一直食緊
Past I ate
當時我食		 I was eating
當時我食緊		 I had eaten
當時我食咗		 I had been eating
當時我一直食緊
Future I will eat
到時我會食		 I will be eating

3X3你掌握好未：Present/Past/Future × Simple/Continuous/Perfect？ 新手要先練好呢9種（表中淺藍色標示）。Perfect Continuous 可以之後先學。

新手溫習時式小貼士：

第一步：決定3式入面用邊式(Simple/Continuous/Perfect)

先用 Present Tense講/寫出來

  • Simple：就咁用動詞，冇特別狀態。例如：eat（食）、go（去）、study（讀書）
  • Continuous：動作進行緊，有「緊」字感覺。例如：eating（食緊）、going（去緊）、studying（讀緊書）
  • Perfect：有完成感覺，有助語詞如「咗、完、哂、夠、齊、盡、好、掂」。例如：have eaten（食咗/食完）、have finished（做齊/做盡/做好）、have done（做掂）

第二步：決定要唔要轉Past或Future

  • Present：保持原樣（I eat / I am eating / I have eaten）
  • Past：轉動詞過去式（I ate / I was eating / I had eaten）
  • Future：前面加 will（I will eat / I will be eating / I will have eaten）*

* will 係 modal verb（助動詞），所以後面嘅動詞要用原形。been 變返 be（I will be eating）。

記住： 先決定 aspect（Simple/Continuous/Perfect），再決定時間（Present/Past/Future）！
